# Day 20 — AI Product Lifecycle

## Objective

Manage an AI capability from problem selection through monitoring and iteration.

## Lifecycle

1. Define the decision and user outcome.
2. Establish data rights and baseline.
3. Prototype the workflow.
4. Build an evaluation set and rubric.
5. Compare model and non-model alternatives.
6. Pilot with human review.
7. Instrument quality, product and system metrics.
8. Roll out gradually with fallback.
9. Monitor drift, cost and incidents.

## Exercise and model answer

Scenario: a prompt demo performs well on ten hand-picked examples. Required next step is not broad launch. Assemble representative and adversarial cases, define graders, capture baseline human workflow and test failure recovery.

## PM decision

Advance from prototype to pilot only when the team can state what constitutes an acceptable output, how it is reviewed and what happens when the model fails.

## Interview questions and answers

Data readiness precedes scaling. Evaluation begins before launch. Drift is a change in input/output behaviour. Rollback protects users. The lifecycle continues after deployment through monitoring and re-evaluation.

## Reflection

AI product management replaces “ship and measure engagement” with a continuous quality, economics and risk loop.
