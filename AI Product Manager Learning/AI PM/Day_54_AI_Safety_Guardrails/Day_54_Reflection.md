\# Day 54 — Reflection



\## What I Learned



I learned how AI PMs use guardrails, risk classification and human approval to control AI behavior.



\## What I Improved



I improved my ability to distinguish between low-risk automation and high-risk actions that require stronger controls.



\## What I Practiced



I applied risk-based autonomy to the AI Video Solution Generator.



\## What I Understand Better Now



AI autonomy should not be based only on technical capability.



It should depend on:



\- Risk

\- Reversibility

\- Impact

\- Uncertainty

\- User detectability



\## What I Am Still Developing



I am still developing stronger judgment around defining the right risk thresholds and deciding when automation should stop and human review should begin.



\## Career Application



This strengthens my AI Product Manager skill set because modern AI products increasingly require balancing automation, safety and human control.



\## Coach Assessment



My strongest improvement is understanding that safety is a product-design problem, not simply an engineering constraint.



\## Key Takeaway



Don't give AI more autonomy than the risk justifies.
