\# Day 54 — Human Approval



\## Human-in-the-Loop



AI

→ Decision

→ Human approval

→ Action



Use when:



\- Impact is high

\- Action is difficult to reverse

\- AI uncertainty is meaningful

\- Incorrect action could affect users significantly



\## Human-on-the-Loop



AI

→ Action

→ Monitoring

→ Human intervention if needed



Use when:



\- Risk is lower

\- Actions are reversible

\- Monitoring can detect failures quickly



\## AI Video Example



Generate script:

Low/medium risk



Generate narration:

Low/medium risk



Modify mathematical answer:

High risk



Publish video:

High risk



Delete project:

Critical risk



\## Key Principle



Do not require human approval for everything.



Protect high-risk actions while preserving automation for low-risk work.
