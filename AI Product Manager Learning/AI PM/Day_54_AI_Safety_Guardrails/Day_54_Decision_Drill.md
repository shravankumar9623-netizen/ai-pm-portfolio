\# Day 54 — Decision Drill



\## Scenario



AI can automatically publish educational videos.



Answer accuracy:

98.5%



Current workflow:



AI generation

→ Automated checks

→ Publish



\## Decision



Do not automatically publish all outputs.



\## Better Workflow



AI generation

→ Automated evaluation

→ Risk classification

→



Low risk:

Automatic processing



High risk:

Human approval



→ Publish



\## Reason



98.5% accuracy still means some outputs may be incorrect.



Educational content directly affects learners.



\## Key Lesson



Model accuracy does not eliminate the need for risk-based controls.
