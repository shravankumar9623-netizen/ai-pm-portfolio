\# Day 54 — AI Safety, Guardrails \& Human Approval



\## Objective



Understand how AI PMs control AI behavior through guardrails, risk-based autonomy and human approval.



\## Core Principle



AI autonomy should be proportional to risk.



\## Guardrail Types



1\. Input guardrails

2\. Instruction guardrails

3\. Output guardrails

4\. Action guardrails

5\. Human approval guardrails



\## Risk-Based Autonomy



Low risk:

→ Automatic



Medium risk:

→ Monitoring / optional review



High risk:

→ Mandatory human approval



Critical:

→ Block or mandatory approval



\## Human-in-the-Loop



Human approves the decision before the system proceeds.



\## Human-on-the-Loop



AI operates automatically while humans monitor and intervene when necessary.



\## PM Decision Framework



Ask:



1\. Is the action reversible?

2\. Is the impact high?

3\. Is the AI uncertain?

4\. Is the decision sensitive?

5\. Can users easily detect an error?



\## Coach Lesson



Don't give AI more autonomy than the risk justifies.
