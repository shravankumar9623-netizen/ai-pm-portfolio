\# Day 37 — Product Prioritization



\## Theme



Prioritization — Deciding What to Build, Test, and Ignore



\## AI PM Concept



AI product prioritization considers:



\- User value

\- Business value

\- AI feasibility

\- Quality

\- Risk

\- Cost

\- Uncertainty

\- Effort



\## PM Concepts



\### Impact vs Effort



High impact + low effort = Quick Win



High impact + high effort = Big Bet



Low impact + low effort = Fill-in



Low impact + high effort = Time Sink



\### RICE



Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort



\### MoSCoW



Must Have

Should Have

Could Have

Won't Have



\## Coach Lesson



Frameworks structure thinking; they do not replace product judgment.

\## Key Learning



Prioritization is not about choosing the most exciting feature. It is about allocating limited resources toward the highest-value and highest-learning opportunities.



\## Interview Question



How do you prioritize competing product requests?



\## Answer



I first clarify the desired product outcome and user problem, then compare opportunities based on user impact, business value, feasibility, risk, confidence, and effort. I use prioritization frameworks such as RICE or Impact vs Effort to structure the comparison, but I treat their scores as decision inputs rather than absolute truth. I then make the trade-offs explicit and select the opportunities that provide the strongest combination of value and learning.
