# Day 09 — Product Strategy

## Objective

Translate vision into choices about users, problems, advantage and sequencing.

## Strategy kernel

Diagnosis: educational content production contains repeatable mechanical steps and high-judgement review steps. Treating both identically creates either inefficiency or quality risk.

Guiding policy: automate structured, reversible work; assist ambiguous work; require expert approval before consequential publishing.

Coherent actions:

1. Start with a narrow approved input format.
2. Produce reviewable intermediate artifacts.
3. Capture edit, rejection and regeneration reasons.
4. Expand autonomy only when evaluation supports it.

## Supplied decision

Option A supports ten content formats with weak validation. Option B supports two common formats with preview, error reporting and approval. Choose B because it strengthens learning and trust at the highest-risk points.

## AI strategy drill

Use deterministic rules for official taxonomies and calculations. Use models for classification or drafting within constrained choices. Route low-confidence cases to humans.

## Metrics

Track completion, acceptance without major edits, correction categories, latency and unit cost. Treat learner outcome as a separate research question.

## Interview practice

Describe the diagnosis, choice, non-goals, advantage and sequence; name the evidence gap; explain why saying no is strategy; and connect each action to the guiding policy.

## Takeaway

Strategy is a set of linked choices under constraints. A collection of desired features is not a strategy.
