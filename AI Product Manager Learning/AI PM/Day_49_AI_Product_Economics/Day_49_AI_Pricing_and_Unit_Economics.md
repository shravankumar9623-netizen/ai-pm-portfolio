# Day 49 — AI Pricing and Unit Economics

**Evidence classification:** CONCEPTUAL product-economics guidance. All quantities below are SIMULATED learning examples, not portfolio or business outcomes.

## AI Pricing Models

### Subscription

Fixed monthly price.

Useful when usage is predictable.

### Usage-Based

Users pay based on consumption.

Useful when usage varies significantly.

### Credit-Based

Users receive a defined number of AI credits.

Useful when the product wants predictable pricing while controlling variable costs.

### Outcome-Based

Customers pay for successful outcomes.

Useful when the value of the AI product can be measured.

### Hybrid

Subscription + usage or credits.

Useful when the company needs predictable revenue plus protection from variable AI costs.

## Example

Model A:

AI cost = $20,000

Human escalation = $750,000

Total = $770,000

Model B:

AI cost = $60,000

Human escalation = $300,000

Total = $360,000

Although Model B has a higher model cost, it produces lower total cost-to-serve.

## Core Principle

Do not optimize for:

Lowest model cost.

Optimize for:

Best product economics.

## Product Question

If a more expensive AI system increases successful task completion enough to reduce human work, improve retention or increase revenue, the higher model cost may be economically justified.
