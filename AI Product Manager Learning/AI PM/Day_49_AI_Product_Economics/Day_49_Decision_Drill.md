# Day 49 — AI Product Economics Decision Drill

**Evidence classification:** SIMULATED learning scenario. The volumes, rates, and costs below are not production evidence.

## Scenario

You are the PM for an AI customer-support agent.

Monthly conversations:

1,000,000

## Model A

Cost:

$0.02/request

Successful resolution:

70%

Human escalation:

25%

Human escalation cost:

$3/case

## Model B

Cost:

$0.06/request

Successful resolution:

90%

Human escalation:

10%

Human escalation cost:

$3/case

## Model A

AI cost:

1,000,000 × $0.02

= $20,000

Successful resolutions:

1,000,000 × 70%

= 700,000

Human escalations:

1,000,000 × 25%

= 250,000

Human support cost:

250,000 × $3

= $750,000

Total product cost:

$770,000

## Model B

AI cost:

1,000,000 × $0.06

= $60,000

Successful resolutions:

1,000,000 × 90%

= 900,000

Human escalations:

1,000,000 × 10%

= 100,000

Human support cost:

100,000 × $3

= $300,000

Total:

$360,000

## Decision

Model B should be investigated as the stronger economic option.

Although the model itself costs three times more, the total product cost is substantially lower.

Monthly difference:

$770,000 - $360,000

= $410,000

## Recommended PM Action

Do not immediately roll out Model B.

Run a controlled experiment.

Measure:

- successful resolution

- customer satisfaction

- human escalation

- latency

- AI cost

- total cost-to-serve

- retention

- failure types

## PM Principle

The right AI model is not necessarily the cheapest model.

It is the model or model combination that produces the best acceptable product outcome at sustainable economics.
