\# Day 58 — Voice AI Evaluation



\## Evaluation Framework



| Dimension | Metric |

|---|---|

| Understanding | Intent Accuracy |

| Latency | Time to First Audio |

| Conversation | Turn Success Rate |

| Interruption | Barge-in Success |

| Tool | Tool Success Rate |

| Outcome | Resolution Rate |

| Trust | Escalation Rate |



\## Additional Metrics



\- Clarification Rate

\- Abandonment Rate

\- Repeat Question Rate

\- Hallucinated Action Rate

\- Average Conversation Duration

\- User Satisfaction



\## Quality Questions



\### Understanding

Did the system understand the user?



\### Latency

Did the system respond quickly enough?



\### Conversation

Did the interaction feel natural?



\### Action

Did the system complete the requested task?



\### Recovery

Did the system recover correctly when something failed?



\### Trust

Did the user understand what the AI was doing?



\## Voice AI Evaluation Layers



Quality

↓

Latency

↓

Conversation

↓

Action

↓

Recovery

↓

Trust



\## PM Principle



A Voice AI system should be evaluated as a complete product experience, not only as a speech or language model.
