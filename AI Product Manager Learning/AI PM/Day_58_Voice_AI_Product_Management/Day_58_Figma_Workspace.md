\# Day 58 — Figma Workspace



\## Design



Voice AI Product Workspace



\## Canvas



1440 × 900



\## Layout



Three-column product workspace.



\### Left — Conversation Timeline



Show:



\- Timestamp

\- Speaker

\- Event

\- Tool action



\### Center — Live Conversation



Show:



USER

"Where is my order?"



AI

"Let me check..."



TOOL

Order API



AI

"Your order is in transit."



\### Right — AI Metrics



Show:



\- Time to First Audio

\- Intent Accuracy

\- Tool Latency

\- Barge-in Success

\- Resolution Rate



\## Product Objective



Demonstrate that Voice AI is an end-to-end conversational system rather than simply a microphone and chatbot.



\## Core Flow



User

↓

Listen

↓

Understand

↓

Reason

↓

Act

↓

Respond
