# Day 10 — Product Roadmapping

## Objective

Plan learning and value delivery without turning uncertain dates into commitments.

## Outcome roadmap

| Horizon | Outcome | Evidence gate |
|---|---|---|
| Now | Users can submit valid inputs and understand errors | Task completion and validation failures |
| Next | Reviewers can assess and correct generated drafts | Acceptance, edits and rejection reasons |
| Later | Repeated low-risk steps can be automated safely | Stable quality, cost and exception rate |

## PM decision

Sequence ingestion and review before autonomous publishing. This creates instrumentation and evidence needed for later decisions.

## Exercise and answer

Backlog: mobile app, confidence display, batch upload, auto-publish, audit log and theme customization. Constraints: two engineers, eight weeks, regulated content.

Answer: prioritize batch upload, confidence/exception handling and audit log. Defer auto-publish. Mobile and themes depend on demonstrated need. Express milestones as outcomes with acceptance criteria.

## Dependency reasoning

Evaluation requires captured outputs and reviewer decisions. Automation therefore depends on instrumentation, not merely model availability.

## Interview practice

Explain outcome versus feature roadmaps, handling stakeholder dates, confidence levels, dependency mapping and how evidence changes sequencing.

## Reflection

A roadmap communicates intent and uncertainty. It should make the logic of sequence visible rather than pretending every future item is equally certain.
