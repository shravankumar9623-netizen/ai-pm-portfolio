## Capability 8 — Human-in-the-Loop AI

The AI Video Solution workflow intentionally retains human review.

The principle is:

AI generates or assists.

Human evaluates.

Human edits when required.

Human decides whether output is acceptable.

This supports trust, quality and domain expertise.

## Core Learning

The strongest AI PM capability is not simply knowing AI.

It is knowing how to translate AI capability into a reliable product workflow.
