# Day 48 — Reflection

## What I Learned

Today I learned that professional positioning follows many of the same principles as product positioning.

A recruiter is the audience, and the resume is the product interface through which my professional value is communicated.

I learned to connect:

Capability

→ Product Application

→ Evidence

→ Outcome

I also learned that AI PM positioning should demonstrate product judgment rather than simply listing AI technologies.

## What Improved

I can now distinguish between:

- A product capability

- A product decision

- An implementation

- An evaluation framework

- A measurable outcome

I also have a clearer understanding of which parts of my product experience are strong evidence for an AI PM resume.

## Strongest Evidence

The strongest verified technical/product evidence currently includes workflow automation and implementation work such as the Leaderboard Generator and NGMC CheckIn platform, subject to public disclosure requirements.

The AI Video Solution Generator provides strong AI PM learning and product-story material, but its public case study must continue to distinguish conceptual, simulated and unverified evidence from verified implementation and outcomes.

## What Is Still Missing

- Verified professional chronology

- Final verified education information

- Final quantified achievements

- Public-disclosure confirmation for internal products

- Verified outcome measurements for selected AI product claims

- Final ATS resume

- Final LinkedIn profile positioning

- Strong behavioral and leadership stories

## Key Lesson

My resume should not try to make every claim sound impressive.

It should make every important claim trustworthy.

## Coach Takeaway

A strong AI PM resume is an evidence-compression system.

It compresses years of product work into a small number of statements while preserving:

Problem

→ Decision

→ Execution

→ Evidence

→ Impact
