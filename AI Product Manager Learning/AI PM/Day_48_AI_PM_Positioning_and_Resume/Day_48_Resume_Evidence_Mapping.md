# Day 48 — Resume Evidence Mapping

## Objective

Map product experience to evidence-backed resume content.

## Product 1 — AI Video Solution Generator

### Product Problem

Content-production workflows can contain repetitive manual steps involving source understanding, solution preparation, visual planning, video generation and review.

### Product Direction

An AI-assisted workflow was designed to automate repetitive parts of the process while retaining human review.

### Product Decisions

- Retain human review.

- Support batch/high-volume processing.

- Enable regeneration/editing.

- Evaluate AI output quality.

- Instrument workflow behavior.

### AI PM Evidence

- AI workflow design

- Human-in-the-loop

- AI evaluation

- Product analytics

- Experimentation

- Workflow automation

### Evidence Classification

Product architecture and learning artifacts:

AVAILABLE

Some quantitative metrics:

SIMULATED / UNVERIFIED

Production implementation and outcomes:

NOT PUBLICLY VERIFIED IN THE CURRENT PORTFOLIO

### Resume Rule

Do not claim simulated productivity or accuracy numbers as production results.

# Resume Evidence Hierarchy

Priority 1:

Verified implementation and ownership

Priority 2:

Verified product decisions

Priority 3:

Verified measurable outcomes

Priority 4:

Documented conceptual work

Priority 5:

Simulated learning exercises

Simulated learning exercises should not be presented as professional achievements.

---

# Resume Writing Principle

Every strong bullet should answer at least two of these:

- What problem?

- What did I do?

- What decision did I make?

- What changed?

- What evidence exists?

The strongest bullets answer three or more.
