# Day 48 — Decision Drill

## Scenario

A recruiter asks:

"Tell me about an AI product you worked on."

## Weak Answer

"I worked on an AI video generation tool that automatically creates videos."

## Problems With This Answer

- Does not explain the user problem.

- Does not explain the product decision.

- Does not demonstrate AI PM judgment.

- Does not explain evaluation.

- Overstates automation.

- Does not distinguish verified and unverified outcomes.

## Strong Answer

"I worked on an AI-assisted video production workflow designed to reduce repetitive content-production effort while keeping faculty expertise in the loop. The workflow covers source extraction, solution generation, video creation, review, editing and regeneration. A key product decision was to retain human review rather than pursue full autonomous publishing because academic accuracy and user control were important. I also focused on evaluation and workflow instrumentation so that quality, acceptance, editing and regeneration could be measured. I distinguish simulated learning metrics from outcomes that have actually been verified."

## Why This Answer Is Stronger

It communicates:

- User problem

- AI workflow

- Product decision

- Risk management

- Human-in-the-loop design

- Evaluation

- Analytics

- Evidence discipline

## Product Management Lesson

A strong interview answer is not a feature description.

It is a compressed product decision story.

## Decision Framework

Problem

→ Context

→ Options

→ Trade-off

→ Decision

→ Evidence

→ Result

→ Learning

This framework should be reused throughout AI PM interviews.
