# Day 48 — Resume Positioning

## Professional Direction

AI Product Manager / Product Manager

## Core Positioning

Product professional focused on building AI-powered products, workflow automation and data-driven product experiences, with emphasis on product discovery, experimentation, AI evaluation, analytics and human-in-the-loop systems.

## Selected Product Evidence

### AI Video Solution Generator

AI-assisted content-production workflow focused on reducing repetitive work while retaining human review and quality control.

### Leaderboard Generator

Automation product for spreadsheet-driven ranking, analytics and editable presentation generation.

### NGMC CheckIn / Workforce Operations Platform

Operational product covering attendance, time tracking, projects, approvals, analytics and reporting, with technical implementation evidence.

## Evidence Discipline

Professional resume claims must use verified information.

Simulated learning scenarios are excluded from achievement claims.

Unverified outcomes are not presented as established results.

Confidential implementation details are excluded or generalized.

## Resume Positioning Principle

The resume should communicate:

"I can identify product problems, make product decisions, work with AI and technical systems, measure outcomes, and iterate based on evidence."

It should not merely communicate:

"I know many tools."
