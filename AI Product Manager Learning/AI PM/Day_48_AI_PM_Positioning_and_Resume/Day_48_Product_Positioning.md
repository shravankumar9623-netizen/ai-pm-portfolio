# Day 48 — Product Positioning

## Objective

Product positioning explains who a product serves, what problem it solves, and why the product is valuable.

The same principle applies to professional positioning.

A Product Manager's resume should quickly communicate:

1. Who I am

2. What product problems I solve

3. What I have built

4. How I make product decisions

5. What evidence supports my experience

## Professional Positioning Principle

A strong AI Product Manager profile should not simply list AI technologies.

It should connect:

Capability → Product Application → Evidence → Outcome

For example:

AI Product Evaluation

→ Define evaluation criteria

→ Measure accuracy, relevance, groundedness and acceptance

→ Use evidence to make product decisions

## Key Principle

A resume should not attempt to prove everything.

It should surface the strongest evidence that demonstrates product judgment.

## Resume Test

A recruiter should understand within approximately 30–60 seconds:

- My professional direction

- My product capabilities

- My strongest products

- My AI PM depth

- My technical collaboration capability

## Evidence Rule

I will not present simulated learning metrics as real product outcomes.

I will distinguish:

- VERIFIED

- SIMULATED

- CONCEPTUAL

- UNVERIFIED

- CONFIDENTIAL

This evidence discipline is part of my product-management approach.
