\# Day 57 — Multimodal Product Management



\## Objective



Understand how AI products use multiple modalities to improve user outcomes.



\## Common Modalities



\- Text

\- Image

\- Audio

\- Video

\- Documents

\- Structured data



\## Multimodal Input



AI receives multiple types of information and reasons across them.



\## Multimodal Output



AI produces multiple types of output for the user.



\## Modality-Outcome Fit



The PM should ask:



Does this modality materially improve the user outcome?



\## Product Principle



More modalities do not automatically create a better product.



\## Evaluation



Evaluate:



\- Accuracy

\- Relevance

\- Cross-modal consistency

\- Timing

\- Latency

\- Cost

\- Accessibility

\- User control



\## Key Takeaway



Choose modalities based on user outcomes, not novelty.
