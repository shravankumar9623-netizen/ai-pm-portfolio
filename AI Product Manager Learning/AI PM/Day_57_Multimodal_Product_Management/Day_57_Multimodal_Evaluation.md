\# Day 57 — Multimodal Evaluation



\## Evaluation Dimensions



\### Input Accuracy



Did the AI correctly understand the input?



\### Cross-Modal Consistency



Do text, image, audio and video communicate the same information?



\### Relevance



Does the modality help the user?



\### Timing



Are visual and audio elements synchronized?



\### Quality



Is the output understandable?



\### Latency



How long does generation take?



\### Cost



What is the incremental cost?



\### Accessibility



Can users with different needs consume the experience?



\### Control



Can users select or change modalities?
