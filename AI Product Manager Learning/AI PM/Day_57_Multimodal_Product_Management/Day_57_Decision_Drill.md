\# Day 57 — Decision Drill



\## Scenario



An AI learning product currently provides text explanations.



The team proposes adding AI-generated video to every answer.



\## Decision



Do not automatically add video to every answer.



\## Questions



1\. What user problem does video solve?

2\. Which users need it?

3\. Does video improve comprehension?

4\. Can the improvement be measured?

5\. What is the cost?

6\. What latency does it introduce?

7\. Do users actually want it?

8\. Could a diagram solve the problem more efficiently?



\## Framework



User Problem

→ Modality Hypothesis

→ Experiment

→ Outcome Measurement

→ Scale if Valuable



\## Key Lesson



Modality should be selected based on user outcome, not novelty.
