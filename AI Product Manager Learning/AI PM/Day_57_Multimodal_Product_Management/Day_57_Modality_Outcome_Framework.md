\# Day 57 — Modality Outcome Framework



\## Framework



User Problem

↓

Required Information

↓

Best Modality

↓

User Experience

↓

Outcome



\## Questions



1\. What problem are we solving?

2\. Which modality best solves it?

3\. Does another modality work better?

4\. What does the modality cost?

5\. What latency does it introduce?

6\. Does it improve comprehension or task completion?

7\. Can users choose the modality?



\## Product Principle



Do not add a modality unless it creates meaningful user value.
