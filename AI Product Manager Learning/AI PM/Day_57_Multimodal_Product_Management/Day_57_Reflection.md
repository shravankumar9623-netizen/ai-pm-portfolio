\# Day 57 — Reflection



\## What I Learned



I learned how multimodal AI combines text, images, audio, video and other information types to create richer product experiences.



\## What I Improved



I improved my ability to evaluate modalities based on user outcomes rather than simply adding new AI capabilities.



\## What I Practiced



I mapped modalities to the AI Video Solution Generator and evaluated where text, images, audio and video create different types of value.



\## What I Understand Better Now



I understand that multimodal product design requires consideration of:



\- Accuracy

\- Cross-modal consistency

\- Timing

\- Latency

\- Cost

\- Accessibility

\- User control



\## What I Am Still Developing



I am still developing deeper evaluation methods for multimodal systems, particularly how to measure whether a modality actually improves comprehension and task completion.



\## Career Application



Multimodal AI is directly relevant to AI Product Management because PMs increasingly need to decide how different input and output modalities combine into one coherent user experience.



\## Coach Assessment



My strongest improvement is moving from "What can AI generate?" to "Which modality creates the best user outcome?"



\## Key Takeaway



Choose modalities based on user outcomes, not novelty.
