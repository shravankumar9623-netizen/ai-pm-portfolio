# Day 12 — MVP and Scope

## Objective

Define the smallest coherent product test, not the smallest collection of features.

## MVP framing

For an AI-assisted video workflow, the hypothesis is that structured draft generation plus expert review can reduce repetitive production work without unacceptable quality loss.

MVP scope:

- One approved question/solution format
- Draft script and step structure
- Visible source association
- Faculty edit, approve and reject actions
- Failure reason capture
- Export only after approval

Non-goals:

- Autonomous publishing
- All subjects and languages
- Claims of learning improvement
- Personalized recommendations

## Exercise and answer

Given ten requested features, remove social sharing, recommendation feeds, custom avatars, public templates and autonomous publishing. Retain validated input, draft creation, review, correction capture and controlled output because together they test the central value and trust assumptions.

## Acceptance criteria

The system rejects unsupported inputs clearly, produces a reviewable draft, records reviewer disposition and prevents publishing without approval.

## Interview practice

Define MVP versus prototype, state the hypothesis, explain non-goals, identify the riskiest assumption and describe the evidence required to expand scope.

## Takeaway

An MVP is complete enough to test a value proposition safely. Removing the control that makes an AI workflow trustworthy is not simplification; it invalidates the test.
