# Day 08 — Product Vision

## Objective

Create an enduring direction without disguising a roadmap as a vision.

## Vision exercise

Educational content teams should be able to transform approved academic material into trustworthy, reviewable learning experiences without repeating mechanical production work.

This vision does not promise full autonomy. It preserves expert judgement and allows multiple solutions—tagging support, concept identification, draft generation, review and publishing workflows.

## Strategic boundaries

- Optimize for trustworthy throughput, not maximum generation volume.
- Keep authoritative taxonomies and source material explicit.
- Preserve human approval for consequential output.
- Measure acceptance, correction and failure reasons.
- Avoid claims about learner outcomes without research.

## Exercise and model answer

Scenario: a team proposes the vision “Build an AI video generator with RAG and agents.”

Answer: this is a solution description. A stronger vision describes the user progress: “Enable subject experts to turn approved learning material into consistent, reviewable explanations while retaining editorial control.” RAG and agents remain implementation choices.

## PM decision

Use the vision to reject unrelated automation that increases content volume without improving trust or reviewability.

## Interview practice

Answer how vision differs from mission, strategy and roadmap; how it guides trade-offs; when it should change; how evidence shapes it; and why technology names usually do not belong in it.

## Reflection

A vision should survive architecture changes. It aligns teams around the future user state and the principles that cannot be traded away casually.
