\# Day 44 — AI Evaluation Rubric



\## Evaluation Scale



1 = Poor

2 = Major problems

3 = Acceptable with issues

4 = Good

5 = Excellent



\## Primary Dimensions



\### Groundedness

Does the generated content remain supported by the provided source material?



\### Accuracy

Is the generated information factually correct?



\## Secondary Dimensions



\### Relevance

Does the output directly address the intended task?



\### Completeness

Does the output cover the important information required for the task?



\## Product Requirement



Educational content must remain trustworthy and appropriately grounded in source material.



\## Important Principle



Evaluation scores must be interpreted in product context.



A score is not automatically a launch decision.
