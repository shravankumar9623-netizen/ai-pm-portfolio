\# Day 53 — Reflection



\## What I Learned



I learned that trust in AI products should be designed through transparency, context, verification, appropriate uncertainty, user control and feedback.



\## What I Improved



I improved my ability to think about AI UX as a complete product experience rather than only the AI-generated answer.



\## What I Practiced



I applied trust-calibration thinking to the AI Video Solution Generator, particularly around source verification, faculty review, ambiguity and user controls.



\## What I Understand Better Now



I understand the difference between:



\- Aggregate AI accuracy

\- Individual-output reliability

\- User confidence

\- Appropriate trust



\## What I Am Still Developing



I am still developing stronger judgment around which trust signals genuinely help users and which could create false confidence.



\## Career Application



This strengthens my AI Product Manager positioning because AI UX requires balancing model capability with user understanding, verification and control.



\## Coach Assessment



My strongest improvement is connecting:



Observability

→ Reliability

→ Trust



I am increasingly thinking about AI as a complete product system rather than an isolated AI feature.



\## Key Takeaway



Good AI UX does not ask users to blindly trust the model.



It gives users the information and control needed to trust it appropriately.
