# Day 43 — Hypothesis

## Hypothesis

We believe that providing a source-linked AI draft with edit, reject and approve controls will reduce median end-to-end preparation time while maintaining acceptable content quality because reviewers can begin from a structured draft and verify every claim against approved material.

## Expected outcome

In a simulated practice run, median time falls from 120 to 65 minutes, reviewer acceptance reaches 78%, and measured content accuracy moves from 94% to 91%.

**Simulated learning data — created for PM practice. These figures are not production results.**

## Falsification

Reject or materially revise the hypothesis if total review effort does not improve, accuracy breaches the agreed guardrail, false acceptance appears, or reviewers cannot explain why they approved output.

## Learning goal

The experiment must determine whether the entire workflow improves—not whether the model can generate a plausible draft.
