# Day 43 — Riskiest Assumption

## Product

AI Video Solution Generator.

## Selected assumption

Faculty reviewers can obtain a useful first draft from approved source material without spending more total effort verifying and correcting it than the existing manual workflow.

This desirability-and-usability assumption is riskiest because faster generation has no product value if review and rework absorb the gain. It also determines whether a supervised MVP deserves further investment.

## Evidence status

The portfolio documents the workflow and decision rationale, but contains no verified baseline study or production measurement. The assumption therefore remains UNVERIFIED.

## Test

Compare the same representative tasks in manual and AI-assisted workflows. Record total time, major edits, acceptance, failure categories and reviewer confidence. Keep source, reviewer instructions and task difficulty consistent.

## Decision rule

Continue only if total effort improves without breaching predefined correctness and false-acceptance guardrails. Faster draft creation alone is insufficient.
