# Day 43 — Experiment Design

## Scenario

Evaluate source-linked AI drafting with five to eight reviewers using representative content tasks.

## Design

Each participant completes matched manual and assisted tasks in counterbalanced order. Use approved sources, one rubric and versioned model/prompt configuration. Record task time, acceptance, material edits, accuracy, regeneration, latency and cost.

## Decision rules

- Continue: meaningful total-time improvement with quality within the agreed guardrail.
- Change: productivity improves but quality, rework or cost breaches a guardrail.
- Stop: no workflow improvement or unacceptable failure severity.

## Analysis plan

Report medians and individual task results. Review every rejected output by failure category. Treat the supplied Day 43 numbers as simulated practice, not measured evidence.

## PM takeaway

The experiment tests a product workflow and decision, not a model demonstration.
