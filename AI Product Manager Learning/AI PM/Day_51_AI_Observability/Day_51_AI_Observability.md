\# Day 51 — AI Observability \& Production Monitoring



\## Objective



Understand how to monitor AI products after deployment and identify product, model, workflow and user-outcome failures.



\## Core Principle



A successful AI product is not only built correctly. It must continue to perform correctly in production.



\## Monitoring vs Observability



Monitoring answers:



"Is something wrong?"



Observability helps answer:



"What is happening, where is it happening, and why might it be happening?"



\## AI Observability Layers



1\. Input

2\. Context

3\. Prompt

4\. Model

5\. Tools

6\. Output

7\. Evaluation

8\. User outcome



\## Key Monitoring Categories



\### Quality

\- Accuracy

\- Relevance

\- Groundedness

\- Human approval

\- Hallucination signals



\### Technical

\- Latency

\- Error rate

\- Timeout rate

\- Availability



\### AI-specific

\- Model performance

\- Token usage

\- Prompt failures

\- Tool failures

\- Context failures

\- Fallback rate



\### Product

\- Task completion

\- User adoption

\- Regeneration

\- Escalation

\- Successful outcomes



\### Cost

\- Cost/request

\- Cost/task

\- Cost/user

\- Cost/successful outcome



\## Key Principle



Technical health does not automatically mean product health.



\## AI PM Question



Did the user achieve the intended outcome?



\## Takeaway



If you cannot observe the AI product, you cannot reliably diagnose and improve it.
