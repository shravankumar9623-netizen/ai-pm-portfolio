\# Day 51 — AI Product Health



\## AI Video Solution Generator



Product workflow:



Question

→ Extraction

→ Solution Understanding

→ Script

→ TTS

→ Rendering

→ Faculty Review

→ Final Video



\## Potential Monitoring Points



\### Question Extraction



Monitor:

\- Extraction accuracy

\- Missing content

\- OCR issues

\- Question mapping failures



\### Solution Understanding



Monitor:

\- Answer accuracy

\- Solution mapping

\- Incorrect interpretation



\### Script



Monitor:

\- Script quality

\- Pedagogical quality

\- Faculty rejection

\- Revision rate



\### TTS



Monitor:

\- Pronunciation accuracy

\- Audio generation failures

\- Processing time



\### Rendering



Monitor:

\- Render success rate

\- Annotation synchronization

\- Layout failures

\- Processing time



\### Faculty Review



Monitor:

\- Approval rate

\- Rejection rate

\- Revision rate

\- Feedback categories



\## Outcome Metric



Successful educational video delivered and approved for intended use.



\## Product Principle



A technically successful pipeline can still produce a product failure if the final user outcome is poor.
