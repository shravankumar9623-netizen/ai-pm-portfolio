\# Day 51 — AI Decision Drill



\## Scenario



AI Video Solution Generator answer accuracy declines from 96% to 91%.



Technical infrastructure remains healthy.



Faculty rejection increases.



\## Possible Causes



1\. Model change

2\. Prompt change

3\. Input distribution change

4\. Context quality issue

5\. Solution extraction issue

6\. Evaluation issue

7\. Workflow issue



\## PM Response



Do not immediately blame the model.



First:



1\. Confirm the quality degradation.

2\. Inspect representative failures.

3\. Compare against the previous baseline.

4\. Check model version.

5\. Check prompt/version changes.

6\. Check input distribution.

7\. Check context and extraction quality.

8\. Identify the dominant failure pattern.

9\. Define corrective action.

10\. Monitor the result.



\## Key Principle



Diagnose before deciding.
