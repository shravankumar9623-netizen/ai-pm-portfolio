\# Day 51 — AI Monitoring Framework



\## AI Product Health Framework



AI product health should be monitored across:



1\. Quality

2\. Reliability

3\. Latency

4\. Cost

5\. User behavior

6\. AI-specific failures

7\. Business/product outcomes



\## Quality Monitoring



Examples:



\- Answer accuracy

\- Script quality

\- Relevance

\- Human approval

\- Rejection rate



\## Reliability Monitoring



Examples:



\- Error rate

\- Timeout rate

\- Pipeline failure rate

\- Tool failure rate



\## Latency Monitoring



Examples:



\- Average latency

\- p95 latency

\- Processing time

\- Queue time



\## Cost Monitoring



Examples:



\- Cost/request

\- Cost/task

\- Cost/user

\- Cost/successful outcome



\## AI-specific Monitoring



Examples:



\- Model version

\- Model performance

\- Prompt version

\- Context quality

\- Tool calls

\- Fallback rate



\## User Signals



Examples:



\- Regeneration

\- Correction

\- Abandonment

\- Approval

\- Escalation



\## Important Principle



Monitor both leading indicators and lagging indicators.



\### Leading indicators



Signals that may indicate an upcoming problem.



\### Lagging indicators



Signals that show an actual product outcome.



\## Key Takeaway



A strong AI monitoring system connects technical signals to product outcomes.
