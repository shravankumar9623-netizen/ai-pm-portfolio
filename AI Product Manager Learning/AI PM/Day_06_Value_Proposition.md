# Day 06 — Value Proposition

## Objective

Connect a user job to differentiated, credible value.

## Concept

A value proposition identifies the target user, important job, current alternative, promised outcome and reason to believe. It must avoid unverified superlatives or results.

## Worked example

For faculty who repeatedly convert marks exports into review presentations, Leaderboard Generator turns an approved spreadsheet into an editable PowerPoint with explicit ranking, absence and filtering rules. Unlike manual copying, it applies one transparent calculation workflow and keeps data on the local machine. Time savings are not claimed because a controlled time study is not available.

## Prioritization decision

The strongest proof points are editable native slides, deterministic ranking logic, local processing and automated edge-case tests. Visual polish is supporting value, not the core proposition.

## Exercise and model answer

Options for an AI drafting tool:

- “The smartest writing platform.”
- “Draft policy responses from approved sources and route uncertain answers for review.”
- “Generate unlimited content instantly.”

Answer: the second is strongest because it states the workflow, source constraint and human guardrail without unverifiable superiority.

## AI decision drill

AI value should be expressed as reduced uncertainty or effort in a job, while also naming quality controls. “Powered by AI” is not customer value.

## Interview practice

Be ready to state the target segment, job, alternative, benefit and proof; explain why a value proposition can change; and distinguish benefit from feature.

## Reflection

Credibility is part of value. A modest promise with visible evidence is stronger than a dramatic unsupported claim.
