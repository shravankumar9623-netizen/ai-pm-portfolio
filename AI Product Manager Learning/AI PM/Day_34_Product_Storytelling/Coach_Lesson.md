\# Day 34 — Coach Lesson

\## 3-Minute AI Product Story



\### Problem



Creating educational video solutions involves significant manual effort.



Faculty and content teams may need to interpret questions, prepare explanations, create annotations, record or generate narration, synchronize the explanation with visuals, and finally produce the video.



This becomes difficult to scale when hundreds or thousands of questions need video solutions.



\---



\### Users



The primary users are faculty and subject-matter experts.



The secondary users are content, academic, and instructional-design teams responsible for producing educational content.



Students are the final beneficiaries of the generated videos.



\---



\### Solution



The AI Educational Video Generator automates the end-to-end workflow.



The system can ingest question and solution documents, extract and structure the questions, match the appropriate solutions, generate a teaching-oriented script, create narration, synchronize annotations with the narration, and render the final educational video.



Faculty remain involved in the review process so that AI-generated content can be validated before publication.



\---



\### Business Impact



The primary business opportunity is operational scalability.



Instead of relying heavily on manual content-production effort, the organization can automate repetitive production activities and allow faculty and content teams to focus more on quality, academic correctness, and higher-value work.



The product also creates an opportunity to improve consistency across large volumes of educational content.



\---



\### Results



The current metrics are defined as product targets rather than validated production results.



The key targets include:



\- Answer accuracy above 98%

\- Pronunciation accuracy above 98%

\- Diagram relevance above 95%

\- Annotation synchronization error below 100 milliseconds

\- Faculty approval above 90%



The next step is to validate these targets through controlled experiments and real faculty feedback.



\---



\### Closing Statement



The goal is not to replace teachers.



The goal is to reduce repetitive production work so that teachers and content teams can spend more time on educational quality and student outcomes.
