\# Day 34 — AI PM Concept

\## AI Product Storytelling



\### Exercise



\## 1. What business problem does my AI Educational Video Generator solve?



The AI Educational Video Generator addresses the time-consuming and inconsistent process of creating educational video solutions.



In the existing workflow, faculty and content teams may need to manually prepare explanations, annotate questions, create narration, synchronize the explanation with visual content, and produce the final video.



This creates several challenges:



\- High manual effort

\- Longer production time

\- Repetitive content-production work

\- Difficulty maintaining consistency

\- Additional effort for narration and synchronization

\- Limited scalability when large volumes of questions need video solutions



The product aims to automate significant parts of this workflow while keeping faculty review and approval as an important quality-control step.



\---



\## 2. Who are the users?



\### Primary Users



Faculty and subject-matter experts who create educational explanations and video solutions.



\### Secondary Users



Content teams, academic teams, instructional designers, and education-content operations teams responsible for producing and publishing large volumes of learning content.



\### End Beneficiaries



Students who consume the generated educational video solutions.



Therefore, the product has a multi-sided user ecosystem:



Faculty / Content Team

&#x20;       ↓

AI Educational Video Generator

&#x20;       ↓

Reviewed Educational Video

&#x20;       ↓

Students



\---



\## 3. Why is AI necessary?



AI is useful because the workflow contains multiple tasks that require interpretation, generation, and transformation of educational content.



Examples include:



\- Understanding questions from PDFs

\- Extracting structured information

\- Matching questions with solutions

\- Generating teaching-oriented scripts

\- Generating narration

\- Understanding diagrams and visual information

\- Supporting pronunciation

\- Synchronizing narration with annotations

\- Generating the final educational video



A traditional rule-based system would require extensive manual configuration for many of these tasks.



AI enables the system to process unstructured educational content and transform it into structured, explainable learning content.



However, AI should not completely replace faculty judgment.



The intended model is:



AI automation + Human review



rather than:



AI automation without human validation.



\---



\## 4. What metrics prove success?



The current product uses target metrics rather than claiming validated production results.



Key target metrics include:



\- Pronunciation Accuracy: >98% \[Target]

\- Answer Accuracy: >98% \[Target]

\- Diagram Relevance: >95% \[Target]

\- Annotation Synchronization Error: <100 ms \[Target]

\- Faculty Approval: >90% \[Target]



Operational metrics can additionally include:



\- Video generation time

\- Cost per generated video

\- Manual intervention rate

\- Script revision rate

\- Generation failure rate

\- Faculty review time



These metrics should eventually be validated through actual experiments and production data.



\---



\## 5. What differentiates the product?



The product is not simply an AI text-to-video generator.



Its differentiation is the complete educational-content workflow.



The system combines:



\- Question extraction

\- Solution matching

\- Educational script generation

\- AI narration

\- Context-aware pronunciation

\- Diagram understanding

\- Annotation synchronization

\- Automated video rendering

\- Faculty review



The product therefore focuses on producing a reviewable educational solution rather than simply generating generic video content.



The key product principle is:



"Automate production while preserving educational quality and faculty control."
