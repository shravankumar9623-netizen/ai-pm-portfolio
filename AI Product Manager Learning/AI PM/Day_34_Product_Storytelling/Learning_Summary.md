\# 1. AI PM Concept — Product Storytelling



\## What is Product Storytelling?



Product storytelling is the ability of a Product Manager to communicate a product clearly by connecting the user's problem, the product solution, business impact, and supporting evidence.



A strong product story follows:



Problem

↓

Users

↓

Solution

↓

Business Impact

↓

Evidence



\---



\## Why Product Storytelling Matters



A Product Manager needs to communicate with multiple stakeholders:



\- Leadership

\- Engineering

\- Design

\- Sales

\- Marketing

\- Faculty / Subject Matter Experts

\- Customers

\- Recruiters and interviewers



The PM must explain the product in a way that is understandable without unnecessary technical complexity.



\---



\## Product Storytelling Principles



1\. Start with the problem.

2\. Explain who experiences the problem.

3\. Explain why the problem matters.

4\. Present the solution.

5\. Explain the expected impact.

6\. Support claims with evidence.

7\. Avoid unnecessary technical jargon.

8\. Clearly distinguish targets from validated results.

\# 2. Product Story — AI Educational Video Generator

Faculty and content teams may need to:

\# 3. PM Concept — User Journey Mapping



\## What is a User Journey?



A User Journey represents the complete experience a user goes through while trying to achieve a specific goal.



For a Product Manager, journey mapping helps identify:



\- User goals

\- User actions

\- Touchpoints

\- Pain points

\- Friction

\- Opportunities

\- Product interventions

\- Expected outcomes

\# 9. User Journey vs User Flow



\## User Journey



A User Journey focuses on the user's overall experience.



It answers:



> What is the user trying to accomplish, what happens during the journey, where does friction occur, and what outcome does the user want?



\---



\## User Flow



A User Flow focuses on the steps a user takes inside a product.



Example:



Upload PDF

↓

Select Project

↓

Generate

↓

Review

↓

Approve

↓

Export



\---



\## Key Difference



\*\*User Journey = Overall user experience\*\*



\*\*User Flow = Product interaction steps\*\*

\## Product Explanation



Creating educational video solutions can involve significant manual effort across scripting, annotation, narration, synchronization, rendering, and review.



The primary users are faculty and subject-matter experts, while content and academic teams are important secondary users.



The AI Educational Video Generator is designed to automate significant portions of this workflow. It can process question and solution content, generate teaching-oriented scripts, support narration, synchronize annotations, and produce reviewable educational videos.



The business opportunity is to reduce repetitive production work, improve consistency, and make educational video production more scalable.



The current metrics are product targets rather than validated production results. These include answer accuracy above 98%, pronunciation accuracy above 98%, diagram relevance above 95%, annotation synchronization error below 100 milliseconds, and faculty approval above 90%.



The goal is not to replace teachers. The goal is to reduce repetitive production work so that teachers and content teams can spend more time on educational quality and student outcomes.

\# 15. Key PM Principles Learned



\## Principle 1



Start with the problem, not the technology.



\## Principle 2



Understand the complete user journey before designing a solution.



\## Principle 3



Identify friction before proposing features.



\## Principle 4



Prioritize problems based on impact, frequency, and strategic importance.



\## Principle 5



Connect product decisions to measurable outcomes.



\## Principle 6



Do not present targets as validated results.



\## Principle 7



AI should solve a meaningful user problem rather than being added simply because AI is available.



\## Principle 8



Human review can remain important in high-quality AI products.

\# 17. Key Takeaway



A strong Product Manager does not start by asking:



> "What feature should we build?"



A strong Product Manager asks:



> "What is the user trying to accomplish, where does the journey break down, which problem is worth solving, and how will we know that our solution created value?"
