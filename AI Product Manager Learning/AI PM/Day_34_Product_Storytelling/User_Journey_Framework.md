\# User Journey Framework

\## AI Educational Video Generator



\### User



Primary User:

Faculty / Subject Matter Expert



Secondary User:

Content / Academic Team



End Beneficiary:

Student



\---



\### User Goal



Create a high-quality educational video solution for a question that can be reviewed and published for students.



\---



\### Trigger



A question or question set requires a video explanation.



\---



\### Current Journey



Question received

↓

Faculty understands the question

↓

Solution is prepared

↓

Explanation/script is prepared

↓

Annotations are created

↓

Narration is prepared

↓

Narration and visual explanation are synchronized

↓

Video is rendered

↓

Video is reviewed

↓

Video is published



\---



\### Major Pain Points



1\. Manual script preparation

2\. Manual annotation

3\. Narration effort

4\. Synchronization effort

5\. Repetitive production work

6\. Production inconsistency

7\. Difficulty scaling the process across large volumes



\---



\### Product Intervention



The AI Educational Video Generator introduces automation across the workflow:



Question extraction

↓

Solution matching

↓

Teaching script generation

↓

AI narration

↓

Annotation synchronization

↓

Video rendering

↓

Faculty review



\---



\### Expected User Outcomes



\- Reduced repetitive production effort

\- Faster video creation

\- More consistent production

\- Improved scalability

\- More time for faculty to focus on academic quality



These are intended outcomes until validated with measured data.



\---



\### Expected Business Outcomes



\- Increased content-production capacity

\- Potential reduction in production cost

\- Faster publishing

\- Better utilization of content resources

\- Ability to scale video solutions



These are hypotheses/targets until supported by evidence.



\---



\### Key Product Insight



The biggest opportunity is not simply generating a video.



The opportunity is reducing friction across the entire educational video-production journey while keeping faculty in control of quality.
