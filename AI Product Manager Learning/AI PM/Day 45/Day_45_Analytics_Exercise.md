\# Day 45 — Analytics Exercise



\## Scenario



AI Video Solution Generator



\## Week 1 vs Week 2



| Metric | Week 1 | Week 2 |

|---|---:|---:|

| Active users | 1,000 | 1,200 |

| Videos generated | 520 | 720 |

| Videos accepted | 364 | 396 |

| Avg regeneration/user | 1.2 | 2.4 |

| Avg editing time | 12 min | 21 min |

| AI generation cost/video | $0.08 | $0.11 |

| Content accuracy | 94% | 91% |



\## Analysis



Active users:

+20%



Videos generated:

+38.5%



Videos accepted:

+8.8%



Regeneration:

+100%



Editing time:

+75%



Cost:

+37.5%



Accuracy:

\-3 percentage points



\## Acceptance Rate



Week 1:



364 / 520 = 70%



Week 2:



396 / 720 = 55%



\## Key Observation



Generation volume increased substantially, but acceptance efficiency declined.



At the same time:



\- Regeneration doubled

\- Editing time increased 75%

\- Accuracy declined



\## Initial Hypothesis



The product may be scaling output faster than first-pass AI quality.



\## Product Decision



Investigate first-pass quality and downstream rework before increasing generation volume further.



\## Caveat



This is a simulated learning dataset. The hypothesis requires validation using real product data.
