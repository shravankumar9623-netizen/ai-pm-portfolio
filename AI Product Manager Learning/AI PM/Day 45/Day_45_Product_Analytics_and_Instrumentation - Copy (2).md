\# Day 45 — Product Analytics \& Instrumentation



Date: 07-09-2026



\## Objective



Learn how AI Product Managers use product analytics and instrumentation to understand user behaviour, identify product problems, and make evidence-based decisions.



\## PM Concept



Product analytics is the systematic use of user behaviour data to understand what users do, where they struggle, and where the product creates value.



Core loop:



User Behaviour → Data → Metric → Analysis → Insight → Decision → Product Change → New Data



\## Instrumentation



Instrumentation means deliberately tracking important user and system events.



Example AI Video Solution Generator events:



\- Source Uploaded

\- Generation Started

\- Generation Completed

\- Solution Reviewed

\- Solution Edited

\- Solution Regenerated

\- Solution Accepted

\- Video Published



\## AI Interaction Telemetry



AI products require measurement of how users interact with AI outputs.



Important signals:



\- Regeneration rate

\- Edit rate

\- Accept/reject rate

\- Human override

\- AI quality

\- Latency

\- Cost

\- Failure rate



\## Key Lesson



Metrics are signals, not explanations.



A metric movement should lead to investigation and hypothesis formation before product decisions are made.



\## Exercise Insight



Week 1 → Week 2:



\- Active users: +20%

\- Videos generated: +38.5%

\- Videos accepted: +8.8%

\- Regeneration: +100%

\- Editing time: +75%

\- Cost/video: +37.5%

\- Accuracy: -3 percentage points



Acceptance rate:



Week 1 = 70%

Week 2 = 55%



\## PM Decision



The product is generating substantially more content, but downstream acceptance efficiency is declining.



Initial hypothesis:



The product may be scaling output faster than first-pass AI quality.



Priority:



Investigate and improve first-pass quality before simply increasing generation volume.



\## Important Caveat



The hypothesis must be validated with deeper data before being treated as a confirmed root cause.

\## Key Takeaway



The job of a PM is not to report metrics.



The job is to turn evidence into better product decisions.
