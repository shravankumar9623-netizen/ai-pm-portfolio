## Product



AI Video Solution Generator



## Primary User



Faculty / Subject Matter Expert



## Problem



Faculty time was constrained by studio booking, recording, editing, review and repetitive production activities.



## Product Goal



Reduce repetitive video-production effort while allowing faculty to maintain control over academic quality.



## MVP



Question Paper / Source

→ AI Processing

→ Question Extraction

→ Solution Matching / Generation

→ Video Generation

→ Faculty Review

→ Final Output



## Key Product Decisions



1\. Keep human review in the workflow.

2\. Support higher-volume processing.

3\. Include question-to-solution matching.

4\. Allow regeneration and refinement.

5\. Use faculty feedback for continuous improvement.



## Rejected Alternative



Fully autonomous generation and publishing without faculty review.



Reason:



- Academic accuracy risk

- Incorrect solutions

- Reduced faculty control

- Trust concerns



## Evidence Chain



AI Performance

→ User Behaviour

→ Workflow Outcome

→ Product Value



## Simulated Learning Evidence



- 40–50 questions in approximately 2 hours previously

- 200–250 questions possible in the simulated AI-assisted workflow

- Median production time: 120 → 65 minutes

- Accuracy: 94% → 91%

- Human acceptance: 78%

- Regeneration: 3 → 2



## Trade-off



The simulated workflow demonstrates significant productivity potential, but the quality decline highlights the need for further AI evaluation and improvement.



## PM Decision



Continue the product direction while prioritizing first-pass academic quality and reducing downstream rework.



## Important Evidence Note



The numerical values in this learning exercise are simulated and must not be presented as verified production outcomes.



## Key PM Principle



A strong product case study demonstrates:



Problem → Evidence → Decision → Trade-off → Outcome → Learning → Next Step
