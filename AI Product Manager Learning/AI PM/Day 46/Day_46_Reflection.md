# Day 46 Reflection



## What I Learned



1\. A strong case study demonstrates decisions, not just features.

2\. Evidence makes product stories credible.

3\. AI metrics should connect to user and product outcomes.

4\. Trade-offs should be explicitly documented.

5\. Negative signals should not be hidden.

6\. Human-in-the-loop can be a deliberate product requirement.

7\. Case studies should end with learning and next steps.



## What Improved



I can now structure an AI PM case study around:



Problem

→ Evidence

→ Decision

→ MVP

→ Experiment

→ AI Evaluation

→ Analytics

→ Outcome

→ Trade-off

→ Next Step



## What I Still Need to Improve



- Collecting real user evidence

- Documenting actual product decisions

- Validating AI evaluation results

- Measuring actual product outcomes

- Building stronger business-impact evidence

- Connecting product analytics to real production data



## PM Principle



A product story becomes powerful when every important decision has a reason and every important outcome has evidence.
