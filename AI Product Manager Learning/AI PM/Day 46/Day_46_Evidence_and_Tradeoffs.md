# Day 46 — Evidence and Trade-offs



## Evidence Chain



AI Performance

→ User Behaviour

→ Workflow Outcome

→ Product Value



## Simulated Evidence



| Metric | Before | After |

|---|---:|---:|

| Questions handled | 40–50 | 200–250 |

| Median production time | 120 min | 65 min |

| Accuracy | 94% | 91% |

| Human acceptance | — | 78% |

| Regeneration | 3 | 2 |



## Positive Signal



The simulated workflow demonstrates significant productivity potential.



## Negative Signal



Content accuracy declines from 94% to 91%.



## Trade-off



Higher content production capacity versus maintaining academic quality.



## PM Decision



Continue development while prioritizing:



- Better first-pass quality

- Reduced rework

- Higher acceptance

- Stronger AI evaluation

- Faculty feedback



## Important



This is simulated learning data and is not verified production evidence.



## Evidence Hygiene



Do not present simulated values as actual product outcomes.



Real product claims should only use validated evidence.
