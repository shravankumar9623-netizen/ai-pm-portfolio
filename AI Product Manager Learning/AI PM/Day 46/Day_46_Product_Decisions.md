# Day 46 — Product Decisions



## Decision 1 — Human Review



### Decision



Keep faculty review in the workflow.



### Why



Academic content requires subject-matter oversight.



### Trade-off



Less automation.



### Benefit



Greater quality control and trust.



---



## Decision 2 — Higher-Volume Processing



### Decision



Support batchhigh-volume content processing.



### Why



The user problem includes limited production capacity.



### Trade-off



Higher processing complexity and potentially higher cost.



### Benefit



Higher content-production capacity.



---



## Decision 3 — Solution Matching



### Decision



Include question-to-solution matching.



### Why



The correct solution must correspond to the correct question.



### Trade-off



Additional processing logic.



### Benefit



Better workflow consistency.



---



## Decision 4 — Regeneration



### Decision



Allow users to regeneraterefine AI output.



### Why



AI output may require correction or refinement.



### Trade-off



Additional AI inference cost.



### Benefit



Greater user control.



---



## Decision 5 — Faculty Feedback



### Decision



Use faculty feedback to improve the product.



### Why



Faculty are the domain experts.



### Trade-off



Human feedback remains part of the product loop.



### Benefit



Continuous product and quality improvement.



---



## Rejected Alternative



Fully autonomous generation and publishing without faculty review.



### Reasons



- Academic accuracy risk

- Incorrect solutions

- Contextual errors

- Reduced faculty control

- Trust concerns



## PM Principle



Human-in-the-loop can be a deliberate product requirement when domain accuracy and trust are critical.
