\# AI Adoption Funnel



Awareness

↓

Trial

↓

Activation

↓

First Value

↓

Repeat Usage

↓

Retention

↓

Team Adoption

↓

Workflow Integration



Metrics:



Awareness:

Users reached



Trial:

Users who start



Activation:

Users reaching meaningful value



First Value:

Successful first task



Repeat:

Users performing another successful task



Retention:

Users returning after a defined period



Adoption:

Users integrating the product into recurring workflows



Important:



Usage alone does not prove adoption.



Meaningful outcomes and repeated value provide stronger evidence.
