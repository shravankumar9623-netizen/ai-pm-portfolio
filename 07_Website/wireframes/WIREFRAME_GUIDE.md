# Wireframe Guide

## Primary Recruiter Journey

1. Understand the candidate's positioning.
2. Open the flagship case study.
3. Scan the problem, role, decisions, architecture, and evidence.
4. Review product artifacts and skills.
5. Open the resume.
6. Use a verified contact channel.

## Home Wireframe

```text
Header and navigation
Hero: name, AI PM positioning, primary CTA
Flagship case-study preview
Core AI and product capabilities
Selected artifacts
Writing and certifications
Resume and contact CTA
Footer
```

## Case-Study Wireframe

```text
Title, product status, and evidence legend
Executive summary
Problem and opportunity
Customer discovery and persona
Strategy and prioritization
PRD and user stories
System and RAG architecture
Evaluation and experimentation
Roadmap, results, and learnings
Source map and next-project CTA
```

## Review Criteria

- A recruiter can identify the candidate's role and product problem quickly.
- Targets and hypotheses cannot be mistaken for validated results.
- Detailed artifacts are accessible without overwhelming the summary.
- Mobile layouts preserve the same narrative order.
- Every call to action has a clear destination.
