# Customer Discovery – Automated Video Solution Generator

## Problem Statement

Teachers and content creators spend significant time creating high-quality educational video solutions manually. The process involves preparing scripts, recording audio, editing videos, and ensuring accuracy, making it time-consuming and difficult to scale.

---

## Objective

Understand the real challenges faced by users before building or improving AI-powered video generation features.

---

## Target Customers

### Primary Users
- Teachers
- Subject Matter Experts (SMEs)
- Content Creators

### Secondary Users
- Students
- Coaching Institutes
- EdTech Companies

---

## Customer Discovery Goals

- Understand the current workflow for creating video solutions.
- Identify major pain points.
- Validate whether AI-generated videos solve those problems.
- Prioritize features based on customer needs.

---

## Interview Questions

### Workflow

1. How do you currently create video solutions?
2. Which part of the process takes the most time?
3. How many videos do you create each week?

### Pain Points

4. What frustrates you most during video creation?
5. Which tasks are repetitive?
6. What challenges affect video quality?

### AI Adoption

7. Have you used any AI tools?
8. What worked well?
9. What didn't work?

### Expectations

10. If you could automate one task, what would it be?
11. What would make you trust an AI-generated video?
12. Which feature would provide the highest value?

---

## Key Assumptions

- Teachers spend excessive time creating videos.
- Manual editing slows content production.
- AI can reduce production time.
- Accurate narration and synchronized annotations are critical.

---

## Success Metrics

- Average video creation time
- User satisfaction score
- Feature adoption rate
- Number of videos generated
- Faculty approval rate

---

## Insights for Product Decisions

Based on customer interviews, the product roadmap should prioritize:

- Accurate AI-generated scripts
- Natural voice narration
- Annotation synchronization
- Easy editing before export
- Support for multiple languages

---

## Product Management Takeaway

Customer Discovery should always happen before building features. Product Managers should validate user problems through interviews and research instead of relying on assumptions. Building products based on real customer insights leads to higher adoption and better product-market fit.
