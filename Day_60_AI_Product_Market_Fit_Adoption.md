AI PM Concept:

AI Product-Market Fit



PM Concept:

Activation → Retention → Adoption



Coach Lesson:

AI capability does not equal product value.



Core Mental Model:



User Problem

↓

AI Capability

↓

Product Experience

↓

User Value

↓

Activation

↓

Repeat Usage

↓

Retention

↓

Adoption



Key Principle:



A technically impressive AI feature does not automatically have product-market fit.



AI PMF should be evaluated through user value, successful task completion, repeat usage, retention and workflow adoption.
