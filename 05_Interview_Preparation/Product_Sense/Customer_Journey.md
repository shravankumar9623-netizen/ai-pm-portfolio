The easiest way to complete it is to pick one product and map how a user interacts with it from first hearing about it to becoming a loyal user.

Since you're learning AI Product Management, let's use ChatGPT as the example.

---

# Customer Journey Mapping – ChatGPT

## Stage 1 Awareness

User Goal
Learn about an AI tool that can improve productivity.

User Action
Sees a LinkedIn post, YouTube video, blog, or friend recommending ChatGPT.

Pain Point
Doesn't know which AI tool to trust or whether it's worth trying.

Opportunity
Build trust through educational content, testimonials, and real-world use cases.

Product Improvement
Create beginner-friendly tutorials, product demos, and comparison pages.

---

## Stage 2 Consideration

User Goal
Decide whether ChatGPT is the right AI assistant.

User Action
Reads reviews, compares ChatGPT with Gemini and Claude, and watches demo videos.

Pain Point
Confused by different plans, features, and AI models.

Opportunity
Clearly explain product benefits and use cases.

Product Improvement
Provide an interactive comparison guide and personalized onboarding recommendations.

---

## Stage 3 Sign Up

User Goal
Create an account and start using ChatGPT.

User Action
Registers using email or Google account and completes onboarding.

Pain Point
May hesitate due to payment concerns or a complicated signup process.

Opportunity
Make onboarding fast and frictionless.

Product Improvement
Offer guided onboarding, free examples, and an optional product tour.

---

## Stage 4 Usage

User Goal
Use ChatGPT to solve daily tasks efficiently.

User Action
Asks questions, generates content, writes code, or summarizes documents.

Pain Point
Receives inaccurate responses or doesn't know how to write effective prompts.

Opportunity
Help users achieve better results faster.

Product Improvement
Provide prompt suggestions, templates, and contextual guidance.

---

## Stage 5 Retention

User Goal
Continue getting value from ChatGPT over time.

User Action
Uses ChatGPT regularly for work, study, and productivity.

Pain Point
May stop using the product if competitors offer better features or if perceived value decreases.

Opportunity
Increase long-term engagement through continuous improvements and personalized experiences.

Product Improvement
Introduce personalized recommendations, usage insights, new capabilities, and loyalty-focused features.

---

# ⭐ PM Interview Tip

Whenever you're asked to create a Customer Journey Map, remember this simple framework

 Stage          Ask Yourself                                        
 -------------  --------------------------------------------------- 
 Awareness      How does the user discover the product             
 Consideration  Why would they choose it                           
 Sign Up        What prevents them from getting started            
 Usage          How do they experience the product                 
 Retention      Why do they continue using it instead of switching 

This five-stage framework works for almost every consumer or SaaS product and is commonly used in Product Management interviews.
