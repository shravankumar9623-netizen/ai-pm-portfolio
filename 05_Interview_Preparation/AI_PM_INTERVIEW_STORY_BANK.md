# AI Product Manager Interview Story Bank

These stories use only available evidence. Results that are unavailable are stated as such rather than invented.

## 1. AI Video Solution Generator — product design

- Situation: educational video creation was framed as a fragmented, review-heavy workflow.
- Task: define a trustworthy AI-assisted product approach.
- Action: created discovery framing, strategy, PRD, system/RAG architecture, evaluation plan and human-review controls.
- Decision: rejected autonomous publishing and optimized for source-linked review.
- Result: a coherent portfolio design package; implementation and production outcome evidence are unavailable.
- Learning: AI quality must be measured at the approved user outcome, not generation alone.

## 2. NGMC CheckIn — operational platform

- Situation: attendance, time, projects, approval and reporting required one role-aware workflow.
- Task: support distinct admin, manager and employee/faculty jobs safely.
- Action: contributed to a full-stack system with authorization, persistence, timers, reporting, audit history and tests.
- Decision: enforced server-side scope, approval locking, recovery and durable notification behaviour.
- Result: working code and automated validation exist; adoption and business impact are not claimed.
- Learning: operational products succeed through controls and recovery, not dashboards alone.

## 3. Leaderboard Generator — product execution

- Situation: faculty result presentations required repeated spreadsheet interpretation and slide construction.
- Task: create a reliable local workflow across variable sheet formats.
- Action: built mapping, ranking, absence handling, analytics, editable PowerPoint generation, presets and tests.
- Decision: made correctness choices explicit and kept sensitive data local.
- Result: functioning code and validated edge-case outputs; measured user time savings are unavailable.
- Learning: the unglamorous validation and error states determine adoption readiness.

## 4. AI quality versus productivity

- Situation: simulated Day 43 results showed lower time but lower accuracy.
- Decision: change and continue a controlled test rather than broad rollout.
- Evidence status: SIMULATED learning exercise.
- Learning: productivity does not override correctness, false acceptance or rework.

## 5. Prioritization

The flagship MVP prioritizes source-linked review, failure capture and approval over autonomous publishing or visual customization. The reasoning is learning value and risk reduction; real rollout results are unavailable.

## 6. Failure and learning

No verified real product failure with an authorized outcome is documented. The honest interview response should discuss a technical/product challenge from verified work only after confirming personal ownership, collaborators and consequence.

## 7. Stakeholder challenge

Insufficient public evidence exists for a specific conflict story. Do not invent disagreement or quotes. Prepare this privately from a real event using situation, competing goals, action, decision, result and learning.

## 8. Leadership and ownership

Code and documentation demonstrate initiative and systems thinking, but formal team authority and outcome evidence are not established publicly. Describe decisions and work performed; do not imply people management.

## Practice rule

Every answer must distinguish personal action, team contribution, verified result and remaining uncertainty. Each story should be rehearsed in two-minute and five-minute versions.
