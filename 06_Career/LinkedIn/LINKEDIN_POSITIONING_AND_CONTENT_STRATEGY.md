# LinkedIn Positioning and Content Strategy

## Positioning

AI Product Manager focused on trustworthy workflow automation, human-in-the-loop systems, AI evaluation and practical product decisions in education and operations.

## Content pillars

1. AI product strategy: when AI is and is not the right solution.
2. Evaluation: connecting model quality to acceptance, rework, latency and cost.
3. Human control: approval, constrained taxonomies, recovery and auditability.
4. Product analytics: measuring approved outcomes rather than generations.
5. Technical product thinking: APIs, RAG, workflows, permissions and failure modes.
6. Real building lessons: sanitized insights from verified product work.

## Publishing mix

- 40% reusable AI PM insight
- 25% product decision breakdowns
- 20% evidence-safe building lessons
- 10% interview-style frameworks
- 5% portfolio updates

Do not publish a day-by-day diary. Do not use simulated data as product results. Internal product stories require confidentiality and ownership approval.

## Quality checklist

Each post should contain a clear situation, non-obvious decision, trade-off and reusable takeaway. Remove confidential names, screenshots, data and URLs. Link to the portfolio only when the referenced artifact is recruiter-ready.
