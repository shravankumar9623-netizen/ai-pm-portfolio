# Portfolio Readiness Gate

## Ready

- Canonical learning record through Day 46
- Evidence classification system

## Requires private verification before applications

- Exact employment chronology and formal titles
- Education and certification details
- Email and LinkedIn URL
- Ownership/disclosure permission for internal systems
- Quantitative impact evidence
- One verified failure, stakeholder and leadership story

## Decision

The public portfolio can be reviewed, but the application resume must not be submitted until the private verification items are resolved.
