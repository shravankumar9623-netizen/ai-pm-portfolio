# Target Roles

## Primary

- AI Product Manager
- Product Manager, AI/ML
- Technical Product Manager for AI-enabled workflows
- Product Manager for EdTech, productivity or internal platforms

## Role filter

Prioritize roles where existing evidence is relevant: workflow automation, human-in-the-loop AI, evaluation, operations platforms, analytics and education technology. Avoid presenting simulated metrics as qualification evidence.

## Target-company approach

Tier-1/MNC opportunities require a tailored resume, verified chronology, two defensible case studies, strong execution and behavioural stories, and evidence that clearly separates personal ownership from team contribution.
