# About Shravan Kumar

Shravan Kumar is an AI Product Manager with established experience across product management, EdTech, product operations, and AI-enabled workflow automation.

He works from user and operating problems through product strategy, requirements, workflow and UX design, AI/model decisions, evaluation, analytics, testing, and delivery. His product approach combines human-in-the-loop design, evidence-led iteration, and hands-on technical collaboration.

## Selected Product Work

- **AI Video Solution Generator:** initiated and led a faculty-reviewed AI workflow from source material through generation, evaluation, human review, and final output.
- **Workforce Operations Platform:** initiated and defined a role-aware operational product spanning attendance, time, projects, approvals, reporting, and productivity workflows.
- **QPG Tagging Automation:** designed controlled ingestion and taxonomy-tagging workflows with preview, dry-run, and explicit write controls.
- **Leaderboard Generator:** initiated, designed, and built a local Python/Streamlit analytics-to-PowerPoint product.

## Product Principles

- Start with the user problem and operating context.
- Define the workflow, outcome, and evidence before scaling.
- Treat AI quality as product quality.
- Keep humans in the loop where trust and accountability matter.
- Instrument acceptance, rework, cost, latency, and user outcomes.
- Use evidence and trade-offs to guide iteration.
