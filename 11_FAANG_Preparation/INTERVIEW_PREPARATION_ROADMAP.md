# Interview Preparation Roadmap

This roadmap prepares reusable interview evidence and does not initiate or submit job applications.

## Phase 1 — Evidence Foundation

- Finalize the master career timeline.
- Verify every achievement, date, scope, and outcome.
- Build a source-linked achievements bank.
- Prepare a concise walkthrough of the flagship case study.

## Phase 2 — Core Product Interviews

- Product sense and customer problems
- Product strategy and market reasoning
- Execution, prioritization, and trade-offs
- Metrics, diagnosis, and experimentation
- Technical AI architecture and evaluation
- Behavioral stories using verified experience

## Phase 3 — Company Preparation

### Google

- Practice user-centered product design, strategy, metrics, technical depth, and responsible AI.
- Store company-specific notes in `Google/`.

### Microsoft

- Practice enterprise scenarios, platform thinking, collaboration, accessibility, security, and AI adoption.
- Store company-specific notes in `Microsoft/`.

### Amazon

- Practice Working Backwards, operational metrics, written narratives, and Leadership Principle stories.
- Store company-specific notes in `Amazon/`.

## Phase 4 — Mock Interview Loop

- Run timed product-sense and execution sessions.
- Record assumptions, structure, decisions, and missed trade-offs.
- Revise answers based on a repeatable scoring rubric.
- Keep targets and hypotheses distinct from validated experience.

## Readiness Checklist

- Verified professional summary
- Complete career timeline
- Ten or more reusable behavioral stories
- Flagship case-study walkthrough
- Product-sense framework practice
- Metrics and experimentation practice
- AI architecture and Responsible AI practice
- Company-specific competency map
