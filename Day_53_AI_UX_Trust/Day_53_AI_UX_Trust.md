\# Day 53 — AI UX \& Trust Design



\## Objective



Understand how to design AI experiences that users can understand, verify, control and appropriately trust.



\## Core Principle



The goal is not maximum trust.



The goal is appropriate trust.



\## AI Trust Stack



1\. Transparency

2\. Context

3\. Verification

4\. Uncertainty

5\. User Control

6\. Feedback



\## Four Questions Users Need Answered



1\. What happened?

2\. What was it based on?

3\. Should I trust it?

4\. What can I do if I disagree?



\## Under-Trust



AI is useful but the user does not trust it.



Result:

Manual work remains high.



\## Over-Trust



AI is wrong but the user assumes it is correct.



Result:

Incorrect outcomes reach users.



\## Product Goal



Create appropriate trust.



\## AI Video Application



AI generation

→ Verification

→ Source comparison

→ Faculty review

→ Approval



\## Key Principle



Don't design AI to look confident.



Design AI to be understandable.
