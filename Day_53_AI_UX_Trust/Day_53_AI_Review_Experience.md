\# Day 53 — AI Review Experience



\## AI Video Review Flow



Question

→ AI-generated solution

→ Verification

→ Source comparison

→ Faculty review

→ Approve/Reject

→ Feedback



\## User Controls



\- Edit

\- Regenerate

\- Compare

\- Reject

\- Approve

\- Report issue



\## Progressive Disclosure



\### Level 1



Show the result.



\### Level 2



Show supporting information.



\### Level 3



Show advanced controls.



\## Human Review



Human review should be considered when:



\- AI uncertainty is meaningful

\- Business/user impact is high

\- Source information is ambiguous

\- Incorrect output could reach users



\## Product Principle



Important AI decisions should be easy to verify.
