\# Day 53 — Decision Drill



\## Scenario



AI Video answer accuracy = 98.5%



10% of questions contain ambiguous diagrams.



The team proposes:



"AI Confidence: 98.5%"



on every answer.



\## Decision



Do not use aggregate accuracy as individual confidence.



\## Better Approach



Clear and verified question

→ Verified

→ Proceed



Ambiguous question

→ Review required

→ Faculty approval



\## Key Lesson



Aggregate performance does not automatically communicate individual-output uncertainty.



\## Interview Statement



I would not expose aggregate model accuracy as an individual confidence score unless the confidence signal is appropriately calibrated and useful for that specific prediction.
