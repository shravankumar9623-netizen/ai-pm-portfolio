# Feature Prioritization

## Why Prioritization Matters

Explain why Product Managers prioritize features.

---

## RICE

The RICE framework is a data-driven prioritization model that ranks features based on Reach, Impact, Confidence, and Effort to identify which initiatives deliver the highest value relative to the effort required.
	​

---

## ICE

The ICE framework prioritizes features using Impact, Confidence, and Ease, making it a simple and fast method for evaluating ideas when limited data is available.
---

## MoSCoW

The MoSCoW framework categorizes requirements into Must Have, Should Have, Could Have, and Won't Have to help teams prioritize features and manage release scope effectively.

---

## Kano

The Kano Model is a customer satisfaction framework that classifies features into Basic Needs, Performance Needs, and Delighters to understand how they influence customer satisfaction and loyalty.

---

## Opportunity Scoring

Opportunity Scoring is a prioritization framework that identifies unmet customer needs by comparing the importance of a feature with customers' current level of satisfaction, helping teams uncover high-impact product opportunities.

---

## My Case Study

Here are short, interview-ready definitions with an **AI Learning Platform** example.

### **1. RICE Framework**

**Definition:** Prioritizes features based on **Reach, Impact, Confidence, and Effort** to maximize business and customer value.

**Example:** Prioritize an **AI Quiz Generator** because it reaches many students, has high impact, and requires moderate effort.

---

### **2. ICE Framework**

**Definition:** Prioritizes ideas using **Impact, Confidence, and Ease**, making it suitable for quick decision-making.

**Example:** Build **AI Flashcards** first because they have high impact, high confidence, and are easy to implement.

---

### **3. MoSCoW Framework**

**Definition:** Categorizes features into **Must Have, Should Have, Could Have, and Won't Have** for effective release planning.

**Example:**

* **Must:** Video Lessons, Login
* **Should:** AI Quiz Generator
* **Could:** Dark Mode
* **Won't:** VR Classroom (this release)

---

### **4. Kano Model**

**Definition:** Classifies features into **Basic Needs, Performance Needs, and Delighters** based on their impact on customer satisfaction.

**Example:**

* **Basic:** Video Streaming
* **Performance:** AI Progress Reports
* **Delighter:** AI Tutor Chatbot

---

### **5. Opportunity Scoring**

**Definition:** Identifies high-value opportunities by comparing **feature importance** with **current customer satisfaction**.

**Example:** Students highly value an **AI Mock Interview** feature but are dissatisfied with existing options, making it a high-priority opportunity.
