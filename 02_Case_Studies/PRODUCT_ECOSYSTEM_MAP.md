# Product Ecosystem Map

## Verified systems

- Leaderboard Generator is an independent local result-analysis and presentation product.
- NGMC CheckIn is an independent workforce operations platform. Attendance, check-in/out and productivity are modules of that system.
- AI Video Solution Generator is the flagship AI product; local source, tests and artifacts support implementation, while public materials remain sanitized.
- QPG Tagging Automation is an implementation-backed product with user-confirmed workflow ownership and real use; dates and quantified outcomes remain unverified.

## Conceptual educational workflow

The following relationship is **CONCEPTUAL**, not proof of one deployed integrated platform:

Question preparation/tagging → concept and source identification → video-solution drafting → faculty review → controlled output/publishing → operational/productivity analysis.

QPG and video-generation descriptions suggest adjacent content-production steps, while NGMC addresses workforce operations. No accessible integration code proves that these systems exchange data or operate as one production platform.

## Portfolio architecture

- Flagship: AI Video Solution Generator
- Secondary: NGMC CheckIn / Workforce Operations Platform
