## Publication Rules

1. Never convert simulated or conceptual material into a real outcome.
2. Never infer adoption or business impact from source code, packages, or local artifacts.
3. Never expose credentials, private URLs, datasets, user identities, or confidential operating details.
4. Use ownership verbs that match the confirmed boundary: initiated, led, owned, designed, contributed, coordinated, tested, or validated.
5. Add quantified outcomes only after the baseline, method, period, evidence source, and disclosure approval are confirmed.
