# Product Strategy

## Vision

The product vision is to enable educators to create high-quality educational videos within minutes through AI-powered automation while preserving teaching quality, consistency, and engagement. [Source: S01, S16]

The long-term ambition recorded in the source is to become a trusted AI-powered educational video-creation platform. [Source: S01]

## Strategic Focus

The strategy centers on a single education-specific workflow that combines document ingestion, AI script generation, narration, annotation, rendering, review, and export. [Source: S01, S07, S08]

The proposed product principle is modularity: components operate independently and communicate through defined interfaces. [Source: S08]

Human review remains part of the system through the review dashboard and faculty-approval metric. [Source: S08, S10]

## Positioning

For educators and academic production teams that need scalable video solutions, the product offers an AI-assisted production workflow focused on education-specific scripts, narration, annotation, and review. [Source: S04, S05, S07]

Unlike generic AI video tools, the proposed differentiation is curriculum context and a faculty-first workflow. [Source: S05, S09]
