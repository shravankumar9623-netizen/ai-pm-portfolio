# Customer Discovery

The documented discovery objective is to understand how faculty create educational video solutions and identify opportunities for AI automation. [Source: S04]

The target research participants are faculty, subject-matter experts, and academic operations teams. [Source: S04]

The source documents identify manual scripting, narration work, annotation synchronization, repetitive activity, and delayed review cycles as pain points. [Source: S04]

The resulting opportunity areas are AI-generated scripts, AI voice generation, automated annotation, and workflow automation. [Source: S04]

The discovery artifact records a product insight that the core problem is reducing manual effort required to create high-quality educational videos, rather than video creation in isolation. [Source: S04]

The repository does not include interview transcripts, participant counts, dates, or raw research notes, so the documented insights are treated as [Observed] workflow statements but not statistically validated findings. [Source: S04]
