# Product Consolidation Report

## Outcome

Eighteen source artifacts describing AI Video Platform, NGMC Video Solution, and Automated Video Solution Generator were synthesized into one flagship case study named **AI Educational Video Generator**. [Source: U01, S01–S18]

Fourteen numbered case-study documents and one source map were created without editing the immutable source artifacts. [Source: U01, S01–S18]

Resume files were not edited or used as evidence in this consolidation pass. [Source: U01]

## Evidence Controls

Every substantive case-study statement contains an inline source reference. [Source: U01]

All numerical claims are explicitly labeled [Target] because the repository contains no completed validation results. [Source: S01, S04, S05, S08, S10, S11, S13, S14, S16]

Proposed sequencing or expected technical benefits are labeled [Hypothesis] when they are not demonstrated by repository evidence. [Source: S09, S14]

No [Validated] numerical metric is claimed. [Source: S10, S11, S14]
