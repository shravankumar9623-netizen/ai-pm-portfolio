# Problem and Opportunity

## Problem

Teachers and faculty spend significant time producing educational videos while managing editing, script writing, voice synchronization, annotations, visual clarity, and review cycles. [Source: S01, S02, S04]

The documented current workflow is: create a question paper, prepare a solution, write narration, record audio, synchronize annotations, and export the video. [Source: S04]

The existing process is described as manual, repetitive, time-consuming, dependent on multiple tools, and expensive. [Source: S01, S02, S04, S16]

## Opportunity

Generative AI, growth in online learning, lower AI infrastructure costs, and demand for video learning are documented as reasons to explore the opportunity now. [Source: S17]

The proposed opportunity is to automate script generation, narration, annotation, storyboarding, rendering, and workflow status while preserving a faculty review step. [Source: S04, S07, S08]

The initial serviceable segment is described as Indian coaching institutes and educators producing English educational content. [Source: S17]
