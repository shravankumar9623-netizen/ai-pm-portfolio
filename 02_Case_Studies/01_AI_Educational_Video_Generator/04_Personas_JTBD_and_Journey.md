# Personas, JTBD, and Journey

## Primary Persona

Priya Sharma is documented as a senior science teacher with eight years of experience. [Observed] [Source: S03]

Her goals are to create engaging educational videos quickly, improve student understanding, and reduce preparation time. [Source: S03]

Her pain points are manual editing, multiple software tools, voice synchronization, and limited time. [Source: S03]

Her success criteria are professional videos within 10 minutes, minimal editing, and high student engagement. [Target] [Source: S03]

## Additional Users

The broader user set includes schools, coaching institutes, EdTech companies, independent educators, faculty, subject-matter experts, academic operations teams, project managers, and higher management. [Source: S01, S06, S13, S16]

## Job to Be Done

When a teacher needs to produce an educational explanation, they want to create a professional video through one workflow so they can reduce manual production work and focus on teaching. [Source: S02, S04]
