# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product

Automated Video Solution Generator

## Problem Statement

Faculty spend significant time manually creating educational video solutions.

## Objective

Reduce manual effort using AI-powered automation.

## Users

- Faculty
- Subject Matter Experts
- Academic Operations Team

## Success Metrics

- Reduce production time by 60%.
- Improve consistency.
- Reduce manual scripting.

## Why This Matters

A PRD aligns business, design, and engineering teams around a shared understanding of the product.
