# Customer Discovery

## Objective

Understand how faculty currently create educational video solutions and identify opportunities for AI automation.

## Target Users

- Subject Matter Experts
- Faculty
- Academic Operations Team

## Current Workflow

1. Create question paper
2. Prepare solution
3. Write narration
4. Record audio
5. Synchronize annotations
6. Export video

## Pain Points

- High manual effort
- Repetitive script writing
- Annotation synchronization takes time
- Review cycles delay publishing

## Opportunities

- AI-generated scripts
- AI voice generation
- Automatic annotation
- Workflow automation

## Success Metrics

- 60% reduction in production time
- 90% reduction in manual scripting
- Faster review cycles

## PM Reflection

The problem is not "creating videos." The problem is reducing the manual effort required to create high-quality educational videos.
