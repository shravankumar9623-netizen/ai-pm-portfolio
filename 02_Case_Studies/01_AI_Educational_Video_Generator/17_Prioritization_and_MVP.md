\# Prioritization and MVP



\## Product



AI Educational Video Generator



\---



\## Prioritization Objective



Identify which product opportunities should be investigated and developed first based on user value, business value, AI feasibility, risk, confidence, and effort.



\---



\## Prioritization Principle



The team should prioritize opportunities that can create meaningful user value while reducing important product uncertainty within available resources.



Prioritization frameworks should structure decision-making rather than replace product judgment.



\---



\## Candidate Opportunities



1\. AI-assisted script generation

2\. Automated annotation

3\. AI narration

4\. Narration and visual synchronization

5\. AI-assisted quality review

6\. Faculty review and control workflow



\---



\## Evaluation Dimensions



\### User Impact



How strongly does solving the problem improve the user's workflow?



\### Business Impact



How strongly does solving the problem contribute to product or organizational outcomes?



\### AI Feasibility



Can the AI system deliver acceptable results using available technology?



\### Risk



What happens if the solution performs poorly?



\### Effort



How much product, design, engineering, data, and evaluation effort is required?

\## Proposed Initial Focus



\### Priority 1 — AI-Assisted Script Generation



Reason:



\- High user value

\- High production relevance

\- Relatively feasible to prototype

\- Clear measurable outcome

\- Suitable for early experimentation



\### Priority 2 — Human-in-the-Loop Quality Review



Reason:



\- Important AI quality and trust control

\- Reduces risk of publishing incorrect content

\- Supports responsible AI usage



\### Priority 3 — Automated Annotation



Reason:



\- Potentially meaningful production-efficiency improvement

\- Requires validation of visual accuracy and instructional usefulness



\---



\## MVP Scope



\### Must Have



\- Source/question ingestion

\- AI-assisted script generation

\- Human review and editing

\- Basic quality checks

\- Output generation workflow



\### Should Have



\- Automated annotations

\- AI narration

\- Quality scoring



\### Could Have



\- Advanced synchronization

\- Advanced analytics

\- Multiple narration styles



\### Won't Have Initially



\- Fully autonomous video production

\- Complex personalization

\- Large-scale enterprise administration

\- Advanced analytics platform



\---



\## Key Trade-offs



The MVP intentionally prioritizes controlled AI assistance rather than complete automation.



This reduces:



\- AI quality risk

\- Review risk

\- Implementation complexity

\- Evaluation complexity



while allowing the team to validate whether AI meaningfully improves the production workflow.



\---



\## Relationship to Day 36 Hypotheses



The prioritization should enable the team to test the highest-risk and highest-value assumptions identified previously.



The first validation focus should therefore examine whether AI-assisted script generation can reduce production effort without unacceptable quality degradation.



\---



\## Decision Framework



Prioritization decisions should be revisited when new evidence changes:



\- User needs

\- Product impact

\- Technical feasibility

\- AI quality

\- Cost

\- Risk

\- Effort

\- Adoption



\---



\## Evidence Status



All prioritization scores and recommendations in this document are preliminary product hypotheses unless supported by documented research or experiments.



No prioritization score should be presented as validated evidence.
