## Evidence-Supported Delivery Sequence

1. Validate the documented faculty workflow and pain points with research evidence. [Hypothesis] [Source: S04]
2. Build the upload-to-preview MVP using the documented requirements. [Hypothesis] [Source: S07, S13]
3. Add RAG grounding using approved educational sources. [Hypothesis] [Source: S09]
4. Run the documented script-model and narration-provider experiments. [Hypothesis] [Source: S14]
5. Measure quality against the golden dataset and faculty approval criteria. [Hypothesis] [Source: S10, S11]
6. Expand only after quality, latency, and workflow targets have been evaluated. [Hypothesis] [Source: S08, S10, S11]

## Initial Market

The documented initial serviceable market is Indian coaching institutes and educators producing English educational content. [Source: S17]

Schools, coaching institutes, EdTech companies, and independent educators are identified as broader target customers. [Source: S01, S16]

The repository does not support claims about launch dates, acquisition channels, pricing, conversion, retention, or revenue. [Source: S17]
