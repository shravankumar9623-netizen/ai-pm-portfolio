\# Opportunity Mapping



\## Product



AI Educational Video Generator



\---



\## Desired Outcome



Reduce the time and manual effort required to produce high-quality educational video explanations while maintaining teaching quality and reviewability.



\---



\## Current User Journey



Faculty/content teams currently need to:



1\. Review the question.

2\. Prepare the explanation.

3\. Create or modify instructional content.

4\. Prepare annotations.

5\. Record or generate narration.

6\. Synchronize narration and visual explanation.

7\. Review the final video.

8\. Publish the completed explanation.



\---



\## Key Pain Points



\### Opportunity 1 — Manual Script Preparation



Faculty/content teams may spend significant effort converting solutions into teaching-oriented scripts.



\### Opportunity 2 — Manual Annotation



Important solution steps require visual annotation, creating additional production effort.



\### Opportunity 3 — Narration Production



Creating narration introduces additional recording or generation effort.



\### Opportunity 4 — Synchronization



Narration and visual annotations must remain synchronized for the explanation to be understandable.



\### Opportunity 5 — Quality Review



Generated explanations require review for correctness, pronunciation, pedagogy, and visual quality.



\---



\## Opportunity Statements



\### Opportunity 1



How might we reduce the manual effort required to convert solutions into teaching-ready explanations?



\### Opportunity 2



How might we reduce manual annotation effort while preserving instructional clarity?



\### Opportunity 3



How might we reduce narration-production effort while maintaining natural delivery?



\### Opportunity 4



How might we improve synchronization between narration and visual explanation?



\### Opportunity 5



How might we make quality review faster without reducing review accuracy?



\---



\## Opportunity Evaluation



| Opportunity | User Value | Frequency | Severity | Business Value | Priority |

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Script preparation | High | High | High | High | High |

| Annotation | High | High | High | High | High |

| Narration | Medium/High | High | Medium | High | Medium/High |

| Synchronization | High | High | High | High | High |

| Quality review | High | High | High | High | High |



\---



\## Product Principle



The product should not automate work simply because automation is possible.



Automation should focus on activities that:



\- occur frequently,

\- consume meaningful effort,

\- create measurable friction,

\- can be automated reliably,

\- and improve the overall user outcome.



\---



\## Key Learning



A product opportunity is not the same as a feature.



The PM should first understand the user problem and opportunity before selecting a solution.



\---



\## Next Step



The highest-priority opportunities should be investigated through evidence and experiments before committing to additional product development.
