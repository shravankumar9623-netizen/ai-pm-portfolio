\# Visual Style



Use:



\- Clean enterprise SaaS UI

\- Strong hierarchy

\- Moderate whitespace

\- Minimal decoration

\- Professional typography

\- Clear cards

\- Subtle borders

\- Consistent spacing



Avoid:



\- Generic chatbot design

\- Excessive gradients

\- Large decorative illustrations

\- Too many icons

\- Clutter

\- Tiny text



\---



\# Product Design Goal



The interface should communicate:



Search

→ Understand

→ Discover

→ Validate

→ Act
