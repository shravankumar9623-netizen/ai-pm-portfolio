# Day 13 – Product Metrics & Analytics

## Why Metrics Matter

Without metrics, Product Managers make decisions based on opinions.

Metrics help us measure:

- Customer value
- Business value
- Product health
- Growth
- Retention

---

## North Star Metric

A single metric that best reflects customer value.

Examples:

Spotify → Listening Hours

Netflix → Watch Time

Duolingo → Daily Lessons Completed

---

## KPI

Key Performance Indicator

KPIs track business and product performance.

---

## Leading Metrics

Predict future success.

Example:

Daily Active Users

---

## Lagging Metrics

Measure outcomes.

Example:

Revenue

---

## Vanity Metrics

Metrics that look impressive but don't drive decisions.

Examples:

Downloads

Followers

Page Views

---

## Actionable Metrics

Metrics that influence product decisions.

Examples:

Retention

Completion Rate

Session Length
