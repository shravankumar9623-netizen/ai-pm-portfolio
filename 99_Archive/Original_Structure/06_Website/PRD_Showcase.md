# PRD Showcase

## Product

AI Learning Platform

---

## Feature

Save for Later

---

## Problem

Students often encounter difficult questions but have no quick way to save them for future revision.

This results in

- Lower revision rate
- Poor retention
- Frustrating user experience

---

## Goals

- Increase revision frequency

- Improve engagement

- Improve retention

---

## User Stories

As a student,

I want to save difficult questions,

So that I can revise them later.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

✔ Save button visible

✔ Question saved successfully

✔ Sync across devices

✔ Works offline

---

## Success Metrics

Feature Adoption Rate

Questions Saved

Weekly Revisit Rate

Retention Rate

---

## Key Learnings

Writing a PRD helped me clearly define the customer problem before thinking about implementation.
