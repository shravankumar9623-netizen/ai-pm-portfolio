# Requirements Engineering

## What is Requirements Engineering?

Requirements Engineering is the process of identifying, documenting, validating, and managing the requirements needed to build a successful product.

Good requirements reduce ambiguity and help designers, developers, testers, and stakeholders work toward the same outcome.

## Functional Requirements

These define what the system should do.

Example:
- Upload a PDF.
- Generate a narrated video.
- Export the final MP4.

## Non-Functional Requirements

These define how the system should behave.

Examples:
- Response time under 5 seconds.
- Support 1,000 concurrent users.
- Secure file storage.

## Why It Matters

Clear requirements reduce rework, improve communication, and increase the likelihood of building the right product.

## How I Applied This

For the Automated Video Solution Generator, I documented functional, non-functional, and AI-specific requirements before expanding the product roadmap.
