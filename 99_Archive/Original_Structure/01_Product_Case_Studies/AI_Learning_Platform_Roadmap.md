# AI Learning Platform Roadmap

## Vision
Empower every learner with personalized, AI-driven education that makes learning engaging, adaptive, and accessible anytime, anywhere.

---

## Product Goal

- Deliver personalized learning experiences using AI.
- Improve student engagement and course completion.
- Help learners prepare effectively through AI-powered study tools.
- Build an intelligent learning companion for every student.

---

## MVP (Version 1.0)

### Features
- AI Tutor
- Personalized Learning Path
- Save for Later
- Course Dashboard
- Progress Tracking
- AI Quiz Generator

### Goal
Validate product-market fit and onboard the first 10,000 users.

---

## Version 1.1

### Features
- AI Revision Planner
- Smart Notifications
- Weekly Performance Reports
- Streak Tracking
- Course Recommendations

### Goal
Increase student engagement and learning consistency.

---

## Version 2.0

### Features
- Voice Tutor
- AI Mentor
- Gamification (Badges & Leaderboards)
- AI Mock Interviews
- AI Career Recommendations
- Community Discussion Forum

### Goal
Create a complete AI-powered learning ecosystem.

---

## Success Metrics

- 50,000+ Registered Users
- 40% Daily Active Users (DAU)
- 75% Course Completion Rate
- 30% Increase in User Retention
- Average Session Duration > 20 minutes
- NPS (Net Promoter Score) > 50
- 4.7★ User Rating

---

## Risks

- Low user adoption
- Poor AI response quality
- High infrastructure and AI model costs
- Data privacy and security concerns
- Content accuracy issues
- Competition from platforms like Coursera, Udemy, and Khan Academy
- Scalability challenges with increasing users
