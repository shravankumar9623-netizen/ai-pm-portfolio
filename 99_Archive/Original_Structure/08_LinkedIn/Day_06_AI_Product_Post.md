🚀 AI products don't fail because teams write poor code.

More often, they fail because teams build the wrong thing.

One pattern I've noticed across successful AI products is this:

➡️ The best teams spend significantly more time defining the problem than discussing the model.

Before debating GPT vs Claude vs Gemini, they ask:

• Who is the customer?
• What problem are they trying to solve?
• How will we measure success?
• What would make users come back tomorrow?
• What are the risks if the AI gets it wrong?

Technology enables possibilities.

Clear product thinking determines whether those possibilities become valuable products.

As AI becomes easier to build, the real competitive advantage is shifting from model selection to product execution.

If you were building an AI product today, what would you validate first: the model or the customer problem?
