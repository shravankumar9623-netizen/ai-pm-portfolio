# Day 14 – Product Launch Strategy

## What is GTM?

A Go-To-Market strategy defines how a product reaches customers, communicates value, and drives adoption.

---

## GTM Components

- Target Audience
- Positioning
- Value Proposition
- Messaging
- Pricing
- Distribution
- Marketing
- Sales Enablement
- Customer Success

---

## Product Launch Types

- Alpha
- Closed Beta
- Open Beta
- Public Launch
- Phased Rollout

---

## Launch Metrics

- Activation Rate
- Adoption Rate
- DAU
- WAU
- Retention
- Churn
- NPS
- Revenue

---

## Risks

- Low Adoption
- Poor Messaging
- Bugs
- Negative Reviews
- Scalability Issues
