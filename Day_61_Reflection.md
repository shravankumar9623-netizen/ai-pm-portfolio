\# What I Learned



I learned the difference between an AI workflow and an AI agent.



A workflow follows a predefined path, while an agent can dynamically decide what to do next based on the user's goal, available tools, intermediate results, and observations.



The key product decision is not whether an agent can be built.



The key decision is whether agentic behavior creates enough user value to justify its additional complexity.
